About

I'm an aspiring Narrative Designer with a passion for creating immersive storytelling and loveable characters for people to enjoy. I've always loved playing through games and being left with a bittersweet feeling. That's how you know a game is great.

My love for narrative began when I first played The Walking Dead Telltale series and realised how much one decision could spin a story and impact the future of your journey.

At Shiverbug I write the voice lines for our characters, move the narrative forward and make the journey worth experiencing.

Some of my all time favourite games are Silent Hill 2/3, Red Dead Redemption 1/2 and Cyberpunk 2077.
