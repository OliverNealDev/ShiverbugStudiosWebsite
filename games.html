Our debut title

Out of Water

A 2-player split-screen collectathon platformer. One of you is a turtle. The other is a seagull. Together you explore a colourful world full of charm, clever challenges and an army of crabs.

A trailer is on the way. Until it lands, here's a look at the kind of place you'll be exploring.

  • Two players, one sofaSplit-screen co-op made for couch play. Siblings, parents, grandparents, whoever's around.
  • A turtle & a seagullTwo very different heroes with two very different ways of getting about.
  • Collect everythingA proper collectathon in the spirit of the classics we grew up on.
  • An army of crabsYes, an army. Of crabs.
